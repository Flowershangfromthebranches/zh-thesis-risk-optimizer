# Report Safety and Integrity

Report-driven mode must remain an academic-integrity workflow.

## Prohibited Actions

- Do not forge reports.
- Do not invent detection-system names.
- Do not invent percentages, source titles, authors, URLs, or risk levels.
- Do not remove necessary citations.
- Do not disguise source content as uncited original writing.
- Do not crack, reverse engineer, or automate against commercial detection systems.
- Do not promise report outcomes.
- Do not damage technical facts, formulas, code, interfaces, table names, fields, or experiment data.
- Do not rewrite LOW or UNMAPPED mappings directly.

## Required Checks

- Confirm the user provided the report content.
- Preserve report text when it is quoted as evidence.
- Separate report facts from your own heuristic diagnosis.
- Label missing report fields as missing.
- Protect citations and source boundaries.
- Protect technical and experimental entities.
- Output a human review checklist.

## Human Review Checklist

```markdown
- 报告字段是否来自用户提供内容：
- 映射置信度是否足够：
- 是否存在多处匹配：
- 引用是否保留：
- 相似源是否仍有来源边界：
- 技术实体是否保留：
- 是否需要用户补充上下文：
```
