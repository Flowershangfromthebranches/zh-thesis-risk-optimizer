# Report Driven Safety Check

Use this checklist before publishing or modifying report-driven workflows.

- [ ] Do not forge reports.
- [ ] Do not forge similarity sources.
- [ ] Do not invent percentages.
- [ ] Do not remove necessary citations.
- [ ] Do not turn source content into uncited original writing.
- [ ] Do not crack or reverse engineer detection systems.
- [ ] Do not promise detection outcomes.
- [ ] Do not damage technical facts.
- [ ] Do not directly rewrite LOW-confidence mappings.
- [ ] Do not directly rewrite UNMAPPED fragments.
- [ ] Preserve formulas, code, interfaces, table names, fields, experiment data, and reference entries.
- [ ] Separate report-provided facts from heuristic writing-risk diagnosis.
